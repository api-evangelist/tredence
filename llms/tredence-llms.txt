# Tredence

> Tredence is a global data science and AI company focused on solving the last-mile problem in AI — the gap between insight creation and value realization. Founded in 2013 and headquartered in San Jose, California, Tredence builds and deploys agentic and generative AI solutions for enterprises across retail, CPG, healthcare, life sciences, banking/financial services/insurance, telco/media/tech, travel and hospitality, and industrials.
